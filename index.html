Why start with transport?

Transport is not abstract. It touches work, school, family, money, time, weather, stress and dignity every day.

01

People already have a system

Danfo, korope, stops, conductors, route habits and local knowledge already form a living network. We study it before trying to replace it.

02

It exposes real constraints

Not everyone has a card, app, bank account, strong internet connection or time to learn a new interface. A useful system must adapt.

03

It teaches us to build simply

If an idea can work for one person on a basic phone, it becomes easier to imagine how the same engine can serve many different backgrounds.

The big vision is not the first product.

Neuroscience, psychology, mathematics, computer science, electrical engineering, prediction, hardware, research, entrepreneurship and institutions are the wider intellectual landscape.

We narrow the angle so we can test the angle.

Grandiosity becomes useful when it produces a small experiment that another person can understand, use and evaluate.

WHY

Why failure is part of the design

We deliberately make early experiments cheap. A wrong prediction is not hidden; it is recorded.

Hypothesis→Test→Result→Learn→Next test

Principle: minimize the cost of being wrong, so being wrong becomes useful.

What happens after one prediction?

The transport product is a laboratory for a larger builder method.

1

Observe

Ask a real person what happened today.

2

Structure

Turn messy observations into usable data.

3

Hypothesize

State what we think will happen and what could prove us wrong.

4

Predict

Give the person something useful before the journey.

5

Measure

Compare prediction with reality.

6

Adapt

Use the result to improve the next model, question or product.
